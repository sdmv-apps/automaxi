AutoMaxi

Navigation on your XMAX / NMAX cluster

AutoMaxi mirrors Android Auto onto your Yamaha instrument cluster — maps on the dash, controlled from your phone. Independent, free, and ad-free.

Download for Android
— downloads · APK · Android 8+

What it does

Your dash, with Android Auto on it

AutoMaxi streams the Android Auto map to the XMAX / NMAX cluster and keeps it running while you ride. One connection, then hands-off.

One-button connect

Tap Connect once. AutoMaxi finds your cluster, pairs, and streams until you stop it.

Runs in the background

Keeps streaming with the phone screen off, so your map stays live for the whole ride.

Built for riders

Designed for the XMAX / NMAX dash: the map fits the cluster, not a phone screen crammed onto it.

Free & private

No ads. No personal data. Free forever — supported entirely by optional donations.

Getting started

Three steps to the dash

A short one-time setup — the app walks you through it and checks each step before you ride.

Install AutoMaxi

Download the APK and allow the install from your browser or files app.

Import the keys & enable Android Auto dev mode

AutoMaxi links you to the free public Open Headunit keys and shows you how to switch on Android Auto's developer head-unit server.

Pair your cluster, then Connect

Pair the XMAX / NMAX in Bluetooth settings, open AutoMaxi, and tap Connect. That's it.

Keep it free

Enjoying AutoMaxi?

It's free, ad-free, and built by one rider in his spare time. If it earned a place on your dash, a small tip keeps the fixes and new features coming — and keeps AutoMaxi free for everyone.
